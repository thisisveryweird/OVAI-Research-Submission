No formulas were evaluated (both workbooks have zero formula cells in inspected worksheets). This is **processed spreadsheet/metadata inspection, not visual spreadsheet inspection or complete clinical adjudication**. PNG dimensions/signatures only: both chart visuals remain **UNVERIFIED and are not clinical evidence**.
