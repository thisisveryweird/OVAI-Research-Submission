# Builder evaluation and endpoint audit

## Scope and method
Completed static, entire-file reading of **18** `scripts/build_*.py` files (excluding exactly the four `build_tcga_*.py` files assigned elsewhere): 192,626 bytes, 8,845 lines. COVERAGE.csv records exact hashes and lengths (authoritative if totals differ). No project source was imported, executed, fitted or changed; no downloads, installs, network calls or model deserialization. Ingestion-only builders are coverage/dependency evidence, **not substantive model evaluations**. All variants remain in place, indexed below. A preliminary substantive report was persisted before completing source reads.

PDF extracted text pages 3–9 and 16–20 were read fully from `audit/2026-02-08/pdf/extracted-pages.txt`; visual rendering and original source-data inspection were not performed. No `data/` directory exists in this checkout. Counts below are source assertions or PDF claims, not patient-level verification. This is not a rerun of reported metrics.

## Findings, in priority order

### B1 — Historical column identity is assigned, not independently checked (high)
`build_fair_historical_14mirna_baseline.py` module-level extraction reads Dataset 2 header row 2, columns 1:180 (169–188), but only checks its length. It sets `historical_ids = pred['sample_key'].tolist()` (195), extracts measurements positionally (242–273), and inserts those IDs (283–287). Label mapping (295–325) then maps back to the *same assigned IDs*: successful mapping is not independent linkage evidence. `build_full_historical_mirna_baseline.py` repeats this at 207–239, 313–351, 363–401. Its comment describes 119 numeric plus 60 E samples; the code never compares actual workbook headers with those IDs. The two 192-feature clinical builders (`main`, both 91–145) attach prediction IDs to positional spreadsheet columns 1:180 at line 131, then perform a one-to-one clinical merge. That merge verifies uniqueness/cardinality of assigned keys, not their correspondence to actual measurements.

Minimal proposed repair: require an explicit, unique workbook-column → true subject mapping; compare all 179 headers and retained/excluded positions to a independently established mapping before attaching labels. Preserve both raw and normalized keys and document normalization collisions. Do not infer correctness from a high PMML agreement or matching class totals. No repair implemented.

### B2 — Two label/representation families must not be described as one historical validation (high)
`build_14mirna_benchmark.py:325–393` checks metadata row order and adds **current GEO** labels to a nonnegative TPM matrix; its audit explicitly preserves GEO diagnosis (540–545). `build_modern_14mirna_baseline.py:142–166` silently prioritizes `true_label` over `binary_label`, `y`, `label`; it verifies two classes, not exactly {0,1} or agreement of coexisting columns (189–213). Its model is a new L2 logistic model trained on target rows (253–291), not frozen PMML.

The fair/full historical builders use Dataset 2, stated batch-adjusted/log10 representation, historical prediction `true_label`, and assert 97 cancer/82 non-cancer (`fair:335–355`, `full:430–440`). The PDF p3 instead reports the current GEO build as 96 cancer/83 non-cancer, comprising 46 benign/22 borderline/15 controls. Source class assertions alone cannot adjudicate this discrepancy. `build_ca125_plus_14mirna.py:171–216` deliberately discards the benchmark's labels and takes historical labels; its molecular input is still the GEO TPM benchmark. The 192-feature clinical scripts use clinical benchmark `binary_label` instead (134–145). These are not automatically interchangeable experiments.

Different historical batch-adjusted arrays and raw TPM are distinct units/processing histories. New OOF target-cohort models are not frozen-model external validation. Recovered PMML predictions on the same 179 patients, when the original 135/44 split is unknown, must be framed as **apparent full-model reconstruction**, not independent-test replication (PDF p18). Exact 14 feature names are insufficient to assert assay or train/test equivalence.

Minimal repair: freeze explicit endpoint/source/label versions, assert binary values and cross-source disagreements by subject, name each measurement representation honestly, and separate reconstruction from new CV and external transfer in every result index.

### B3 — Clinical linkage can pass with imputed/unmatched subjects (high)
`build_ca125_baseline.py` has module-level execution. `table2_to_actual_sample_id:291–327` maps Table 2 numeric positions onto prediction row order, skips position 56 (said to be 81195), and retains explicit E IDs. This is conditional on independently verified prediction order. A left one-to-one join (386–405) plus 179-row check does not require every subject to match a clinical source row. The numeric parser (362–377) maps unsupported annotations/censored values to NaN. Counts are printed (451–461), but no per-subject measured/missing/matched-source flag is exported as such. CA-125 imputation is correctly fitted per outer training fold (475–503); raw CA-125 is a ranking score, **not a probability**, and the decision uses strictly `>35` (522–526), not `>=35`. Units are assumed, not checked in code.

`build_ca125_plus_14mirna.py:162–196` uses left one-to-one joins, but no merge indicator or missing-miRNA rejection; median imputation can therefore conceal complete molecular non-matches. Its 97/82 count checks (215–219) do not verify subject mapping.

`build_gse94533_clinical_fusion.py` uses Dataset 6 Subject ID/Sample Number (67–80), preferable to row-position inference, then matches Table 2 numbers (83–102). But both mapping dictionaries silently overwrite duplicate keys; unmapped numeric subjects are silently skipped. `main:112–124` validates joins and 179 rows, only requires age >=170 and CA-125 >=100, not complete clinical matching. It writes **gse94533_14mirna_clinical.csv.gz**, whereas the age builders consume **gse94533_14mirna_clinical_benchmark.csv.gz**. Thus this file alone is not the provenance producer for their consumed clinical benchmark. PDF p20's roughly 60 missing CA-125 and one missing age are unverified locally; fusion's lower bounds do not establish those exact counts.

Minimal repair: reject duplicated/colliding mapping keys; export join status and raw clinical strings, units, censoring and measured/imputed flags; verify the actual producer/label source of the `_clinical_benchmark` input; report observed counts before imputation and complete-case sensitivity analyses.

### B4 — Strict borderline feature availability uses held-out samples (moderate)
`build_gse8841_strict_borderline_model.py:41–56` restricts to serous/mucinous, makes cancer=stage_I_cancer, and stratifies by diagnosis×histotype. FIGO, grade, histotype, survival and relapse are excluded from X (58–81): good protection against obvious endpoint leakage. However `missing_fraction = X.isna().mean(); keep = missing_fraction == 0` at 103–107 filters probes using the *entire evaluated cohort*, before repeated splits (138–152). This is unlabeled transductive availability selection, not fold-contained preprocessing. K=min(10,p), C=.1 and 20 repeats are fixed in code; documentary evidence that they were prospectively fixed is absent. Scaling and supervised ANOVA selection are otherwise fitted only on each training fold (160–186). No inner tuning is present.

Its per-repeat AUC is calculated from pooled held-out predictions across five folds (243–246), then mean/SD over 20 repeated complete OOF vectors (295–325). The printed 'Mean 5-fold AUC' (340–342) is not a mean of five fold AUCs; SD across reused subjects/repeated splits is not a confidence interval or 20 independent cohorts.

Minimal repair: learn probe availability within train folds with stable inference feature handling; rename statistics to mean repeated pooled-OOF AUC and avoid independent-sample interpretations of repeats.

### B5 — GSE106817 builders train on the purported external cohort (high framing risk; mostly sound nesting)
`build_gse106817_170mirna_baseline.py:48–80` uses every `hsa-` column, inherited binary labels, and drops incomplete molecular rows. There is no exact 170-feature assertion, nor local metadata source matching. The model is fitted on GSE106817 outer training rows (125–147). Its footer correctly says not the frozen GSE94533 model (318–326), despite 'INDEPENDENT' heading (46): this is **within-target-cohort CV**, not external validation of a fixed source-cohort signature.

`build_cancer_vs_borderline_model.py:59–86`, age version 51–79, and all-miRNA discovery 57–90 select cancer versus borderline only, excluding benign/control and dropping any missing selected feature; age/all-miRNA variants also require measured age. They obtain label directly from `group`. Counts after exclusions are data-dependent and not locally verified. The printed 'Age missing' in the age model (90) is after complete-case removal and cannot describe source missingness.

All three put scaler, ANOVA SelectKBest and balanced logistic regression **inside GridSearchCV's pipeline**, with outer 5-fold / inner 4-fold and held-out prediction at >=.5 (`model:128–215`, `age:105–184`, `all:123–205`). This source structure does not exhibit global supervised feature selection or inner preprocessing leakage. Complete-case eligibility is globally defined, so results apply to that selected population. The discovery variant consumes the same filename and dynamic `hsa-` columns as the 170-feature builders, not a separate proven full-assay matrix (65–70); k up to 300 (139–152) is not evidence of 300 measured inputs and lacks dimensionality gating. Depending on sklearn version, oversized k can warn/use all features rather than yielding distinct candidate sets.

Minimal repair: distinguish target-cohort discovery from frozen transfer; assert actual assay/features and unique patient IDs, save excluded IDs/reasons, and gate k to available dimensions. Require independent cohorts for validation of any chosen signature after inspecting these repeated experiments.

### B6 — Learned operations mostly correctly contained, but preprocessing history and report provenance remain incomplete
`build_age_clinical_models.py::run_model:60–98` and both 192-feature `main:159–202` put median imputation and standardization within each outer training fold; fixed C=1 or .3, no inner tuning. They use raw selected values without an explicit log transform. The age/14 and age+CA-125/14 benchmark's representation is inherited and not authenticated here. Both 192 builders extract published Dataset 2 values as-is; no per-fold refitting of upstream historical batch adjustment is possible from these sources.

`build_modern_14mirna_baseline.py:253–291` scales inside outer training folds with fixed C=1, no tuning. `build_ca125_plus_14mirna.py:244–306` correctly nests median imputation, scaling and elastic-net C/l1_ratio tuning inside inner 3-fold CV/outer 5-fold. Fair/full historical baselines likewise nest scaling and elastic-net tuning (`fair:378–443`, `full:491–554`), despite the fair file's docstring/results calling it 'L2' (`fair:546–547`): its actual grid includes l1_ratio .0,.25,.5,.75,1 and elasticnet penalty (406–429). Fair fold records omit best parameters/inner scores (464–473), unlike the full builder (582–606). Claimed no leakage is only justified for *in-script* fitting, not unknown upstream batch correction/feature discovery or repeated human model choice.

Builders save metrics/prediction CSVs, not an authenticated frozen model with full training provenance. Several omit row fold IDs (age builders, CA-125, cancer/borderline models), selected features per fold, input hashes, software versions, source commit, measured clinical counts, exact label transformation and all exclusion IDs. Historical fair/full and modern add fold numbers. No builder supplies calibration evidence; >=.5 is conventional, not a proven clinical operating point. Seeds alone do not constitute a run manifest. Metrics are pooled OOF ROC-AUC and average precision, not fold-mean AUC or trapezoidal PR-AUC unless explicitly stated. No numeric result is verified in this audit.

Minimal repair: canonical per-run manifests with hashes, IDs/folds, endpoint, source/units, exclusion/measurement counts, inner choices, preprocessing state and environment; calibration and prespecified operating-point assessment in a genuinely untouched cohort. Keep legacy variants indexed, not moved.

## Ingestion dependency findings (not substantive evaluations)
- `build_gse94533_dataset.py::classify_diagnosis:108–159` recognizes borderline/benign/healthy first, then invasive/malignant/cancer substrings; unknown diagnoses abort in `validate_geo_metadata:342–364`. These positive substring rules are not a negation-safe clinical vocabulary. It uses 'Identical miRNAs' / fourth-column canonical names (`load_mirna_matrix_from_workbook:477–550`), sums duplicates and converts invalid/infinite/missing values to zero (562–582). Across workbooks outer concat fills absent assay features with zero (649–655). Those zeros mean neither proven measured zero nor fold-trained imputation. Source workbook is retained in metadata (683–699).
- `build_gse94533_clean_dataset.py::load_metadata:129–184` instead uses exact Cancer/Benign/Borderline/Control and excludes E1056. It groups **feature_name**, not the canonical-name field (`read_expression_workbook:315–401`), also zero-fills invalid values and absent workbook features (347–350,492). Thus these are incompatible producer variants for the **same** `gse94533_mirna_tpm.csv.gz`; original writes interim metadata with diagnosis_group, clean writes processed metadata with diagnosis. Clean collects mappings but does not export workbook mapping/source for final patients. Its count/finite checks do not authenticate that nominal TPM values are measured and valid.
- `build_gse8841_borderline_dataset.py` assigns borderline by grade first, else stage exactly '1' gives stage_I_cancer, otherwise raises (170–184). Blank or unexpected non-borderline grade with stage 1 is therefore positive. Molecular features are tissue-expression probe IDs, not serum miRNA (457–466). It matches GSM IDs and uses one-to-one merge (354–371), but does not establish biological patient uniqueness or repeated specimens. The comment 'Remove duplicate probe IDs' actually checks duplicate **sample** IDs (345–349); probes are not independently de-duplicated there. No explicit log/assay-unit verification occurs.

## PDF cross-check
Pages 3–6 correctly motivate separate cohorts/representations and denominators; their numeric cohort/metric claims remain unverified here. Page 8's claim that borderline is never folded into non-cancer is contradicted by the full GSE94533 endpoint in both ingestion variants and the benchmark audit (benchmark:503–506); borderline is separate metadata **and** included in negative binary labels. Pages 7/16 overstate universal correctness of each individual CV procedure: strict borderline's global missingness filter is a direct counterexample. Pages 18–19's split/provenance/variant concerns apply; no original split is recovered by these builders. Page 20's clinical missing counts, histology mechanism and inability to separate stage I are not proven by these builders; they retain stage metadata but do not implement a stage-I/II or stage-I-only evaluation. Biological plausibility does not verify source matching. PDF roadmap is recommendations, not completed validation.
