`bulk_test_external_datasets.py::main:429–442` ranks reported sample totals; it does not exclude training accession GSE94533 (indeed listed as skipped). `load_local_training:266–271` restricts source cancers to Stage I/II, whereas `label_from_metadata:137–150` accepts ovarian cancer of any stage. “early-stage-compatible” is not an external early-stage filter. Final independent test evaluation is a separate question.

Local GSE113486 and GSE211692 SOFT metadata permit GSM/title checks, not donor independence. Different titles, no exact overlaps or expression duplicates do not exclude shared biobank donors/visits. GSE106817/113740 rosters and linkage are absent here. GSE211692 report lines 99–108 explicitly acknowledges namespaces and inability to prove independence. No new donor-overlap guarantee is made.
