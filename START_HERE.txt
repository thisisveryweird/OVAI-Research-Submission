OVARIAN AI — START HERE

1. cd ~/ovarian-ai
2. source .venv/bin/activate
3. bash scripts/run_public_mirna_baseline.sh

The baseline runner downloads public GSE94533 serum-miRNA files, audits their
Excel structure, tries to identify the phenotype without guessing, trains a
patient-level logistic-regression baseline, and runs a PyTorch fusion-model
smoke test.

Do NOT treat any model output as medical advice or a clinical diagnosis.

After the baseline works, the next development step is to create a truly
patient-linked multimodal cohort table rather than fabricating multimodal rows
from unrelated studies.
